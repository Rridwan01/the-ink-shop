Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • The Ink Shop Private Atelier

1. Age Requirement & ID Verification

Clients must be at least 18 years of age on the day of the appointment. A physical, valid government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, Passport, State ID) is strictly inspected upon arrival before any tattooing procedure.

2. $200 Non-Refundable Security Deposit

A $200 security deposit is mandatory once your consultation is accepted. The deposit guarantees your dedicated studio session and is credited 100% toward the final balance on the day of your appointment.

3. 72-Hour Rescheduling Notice

We require at least 72 hours advance notice to transfer your security deposit to a new session date. Cancellations or reschedule requests inside the 72-hour window forfeit the deposit to compensate for reserved studio time.

4. Private Studio & Appointment Only

The Ink Shop operates exclusively by confirmed private appointment. No unconfirmed walk-in visitors are permitted. The private studio address is provided upon deposit verification.

5. Custom Artwork & Design Review

Custom designs are prepared for appointment day. Sizing adjustments, subtle details, and stencil placement fitting are finalized collaboratively in person.
