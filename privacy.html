LEGAL PROTOCOL • PRIVACY DOSSIER

Privacy & Client Confidentiality

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • The Ink Shop Private Atelier

1. Client Information Collected

When you submit a consultation request through The Ink Shop booking portal, we collect personal contact details (name, email address, phone number, Instagram handle, and age verification) as well as project artistic specifications (reference pictures, placement area photographs, and sizing).

2. Strict Confidentiality & Usage

Your submitted images, anatomical placement photos, and contact credentials are strictly utilized for artistic consultation, project feasibility review, and direct appointment coordination. We never sell, rent, or distribute your private client information to third-party commercial brokers or advertisers.

3. Image Privacy & Portfolio Rights

Placement and reference photos submitted through the consultation form remain confidential. Tattoo portfolio photographs taken on the day of the session will only be published to studio channels with your verbal or written consent, ensuring complete privacy of sensitive body areas.

4. Data Storage & Inquiries

Consultation records are stored securely. You may request the deletion or modification of your consultation record at any time by contacting our private studio desk directly.
