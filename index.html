Studio Etiquette & Policies

How does the $200 security deposit work?

The deposit guarantees your dedicated studio time. It is credited 100% towards the total cost of your tattoo on the day of your appointment.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Providing at least 72 hours advance notice allows your deposit to roll over to an available opening.

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Sanctum operates exclusively out of a private studio by confirmed appointment to ensure a sterile, calm, and private one-on-one session.

When do I see the custom design?

Designs are prepared and finalized on appointment day. Sizing and placement adjustments are done in person prior to applying the stencil.

Can I bring a guest?

You may bring 1 companion for support with prior notice. The studio is a quiet, focused environment.
